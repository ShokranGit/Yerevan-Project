Yerevan Project · a utility page, not part of the map

This packs the 6 photographs of the 23–24 April commemoration — all Creative Commons or public domain, from Wikimedia Commons — into a single file and saves it to your Downloads folder.

Nothing is installed and nothing is uploaded. The images are fetched from Commons by your own browser, wrapped into one archive in memory, and handed to you.

Ready. 6 files, about 5 MB.

If nothing appears in Downloads, look at the right-hand end of Chrome's address bar for a small download arrow with a red dot, or a crossed-out arrow. That is Chrome blocking the download. Click it, choose Always allow, then press the button again.
